CHAPTER I.

The castle stood silent above the valley. She sat alone in the garden, looking forlorn and tired. The evening wind moved the old trees. Nobody came to find her.

At dawn the bell rang once, and the village woke. Old Tom opened his shop and counted the coins in a small tin box. "Will it rain today?" asked a boy. "Perhaps," said Tom, and he looked at the grey sky.

CHAPTER II.

The road to the sea was long and dusty. By noon the travellers were hungry, but nobody wanted to stop. At last they reached a quiet inn beside the river. The innkeeper smiled and brought bread, cheese and hot soup.
