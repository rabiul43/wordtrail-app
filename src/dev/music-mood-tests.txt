## Fine music mood tests. Run them with:   node tools/music_mood_check.js
## Format:
##   === id                          starts a case
##   # lang=en base=dread fine=tension     the book's language code, the scene mood the paragraph sits in, and the expected result
##   Paragraph text (a blank line ends it).
## lang  = en, bn ... (the book's language code; the paragraph's own script can override it, see musPickLang)
## base  = neutral dread danger peace sorrow merry (the scene mood from scene detection)
## fine  = tension wonder romance chase, or none (no fine mood: the music follows the scene mood)
## A fine mood needs two or more word hits, and only refines the scene moods listed in MUS_FINE_BASE.
## The check also runs every case a second time with the letters taken apart (NFD), as some keyboards and files store Bengali; the result must not change.
## Bengali cases were written by a developer, not a native editor: please have them read over (see BN-REVIEW.md).

=== en-tension
# lang=en base=dread fine=tension
The corridor was silent. She waited, listening for footsteps, and a shadow moved under the door.
