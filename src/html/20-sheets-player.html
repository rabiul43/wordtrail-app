How to use Wordtrail

While you read

  • Tap a sentenceJump the audio to that sentence.
  • Double-tap a sentence while it playsTells the app "this is what I hear now". It lines up the highlight again if it has drifted.
  • ☝
    Press and hold a sentenceTranslate it.
  • Swipe up on the textHide the player controls to leave a slim bar. Swipe down, or tap the bar or its handle, to show them again.
  • ↻
    Back to narratorAfter you scroll away, this button brings the text back to the sentence being read.

Around the app

    Heart a bookSaves it to My books.
