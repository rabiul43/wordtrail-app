When does it play?

Sounds start when the book plays and fade out when it pauses or the sleep timer ends.

The music changes with the mood of the story and crossfades between moods. It always plays quietly under the narrator's voice.

    Scene test

    Checks the scene detection against sample paragraphs with known answers. Paste your own cases into the box (the format is explained at the top), then run. A starting baseline is built in (it has no wrong guesses). Save your own once you have reviewed a result; later runs show what is newly wrong. Wrong guesses count against a batch of new words; missed and late do not. Cases stay on this device.
